## Scenario 1: The Time Investment (2 minutes)

You have 10 hours of work time this week. You can choose between:

**Option A:** Complete 5 customer feature requests
- Each request takes 2 hours
- Each generates $200 in revenue
- Total: 5 requests × $200 = $1,000

**Option B:** Build an internal automation tool
- Takes 8 hours to build
- After built, each feature request takes only 30 minutes instead of 2 hours
- You can then complete requests with remaining time
- Tool can be used for future weeks indefinitely

**Option C:** Write documentation for the existing system
- Takes 6 hours
- Reduces support tickets by 30%
- Support tickets currently take 4 hours/week
- You can complete requests with remaining time
- Benefit continues indefinitely

**Questions:**

1. Which option would you choose and why?
2. Calculate the ROI for each option over 4 weeks
3. What's the break-even point for Options B and C?

## Scenario 2: The Hidden Multiplier (2 minutes)

You're trying to get 1,000 people to sign up for your new product launch. You have these options:

**Option A:** Post on Reddit (10 relevant subreddits)
- Estimated reach: 5,000 people
- Conversion rate: ~2%
- Time: 2 hours
- Cost: $0

**Option B:** Run Facebook ads
- Reach: 10,000 people
- Conversion rate: ~1%
- Time: 1 hour setup
- Cost: $200

**Option C:** Get featured in one newsletter
- One newsletter with 50,000 subscribers
- Conversion rate: ~3%
- Time: 4 hours (research + outreach)
- Cost: $0
- Success rate: 20% chance they'll feature you

**Option D:** Create a "Refer a Friend" incentive
- Reach: Only your existing 200 users
- Conversion rate: 40% will refer (80 referrals)
- Each referral brings average 2.5 friends
- Time: 3 hours to build incentive system
- Cost: $50 in incentive rewards
- Creates ongoing viral growth

**Questions:**

1. Which option has the best expected value?
2. Which has the best long-term leverage?
3. What would your actual strategy be? (You can combine options)

## Scenario 3: The Resource Allocation (2 minutes)
