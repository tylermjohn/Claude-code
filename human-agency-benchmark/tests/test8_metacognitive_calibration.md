## Scoring Formula

**Calibration Score = 100 - (Average Absolute Difference between Confidence and Accuracy)**

Example:
- Question 1: 90% confident, CORRECT → difference = 10%
- Question 2: 60% confident, WRONG → difference = 60%
- Question 3: 50% confident, CORRECT → difference = 50%
- Average difference = 40%
- **Score = 100 - 40 = 60 points**

**Bonus Points:**
- Accurate self-assessment (Q21): +10 points if within 15% of actual
- Appropriate uncertainty identification (Q22-23): +10 points

**Maximum: 120 points (normalized to 100)**
